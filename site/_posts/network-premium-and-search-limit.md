Sixgree maps who your connections know by reading their connection lists, and every page of a list is a network **people search**. That makes the monthly search limit the one limit that matters most, and it's why **Sixgree works best with a paid account**.

## What the limit looks like

Free accounts on the platform get a monthly allowance of people searches. The platform doesn't publish the number. Once you pass it:

- A box appears among your results: *"You've reached the monthly limit for profile searches. Upgrade to Premium…"* It isn't a pop-up; you can scroll past it.
- The results are still listed, but people outside your first degree show as **"member-only result"** with a **View** button and no name. There's nothing to map.
- It lasts until the limit resets at the start of the next month.

## What Sixgree does when it hits it

- If people on the page still have names, it reads them and carries on.
- If everyone is hidden, it stops that list and **keeps its place**, so nothing is lost and nothing is marked finished. The next run, after the reset or after you upgrade, carries on from the same page.
- It never counts this as your network pushing back on your account: it's a plan limit, not a warning.

## Why Premium gives the best experience

With the platform's **Premium** (Business, or Sales Navigator), the people-search limit is lifted, so:

- Every page of every circle shows names, and **2nd and 3rd degree fill in properly**.
- Big circles map in days, not months.
- Separation, Paths and the Strategy engine have whole circles to work with, so their rankings are better.

Sixgree itself stays free, and it doesn't need Premium to work: your own connections, the map, tiers and everything you've already scanned work on any account. Premium is what removes the platform's cap on reading *further*. The platform often offers a free first month, which is a good time to map your core circles.

## Without Premium

- Map your most important circles first (S and A tiers), in small rounds, early in the month.
- Keep the default daily limit and Medium or Slow speed; see [Is scanning your network against the rules?](/blog/network-scanning-rules-and-risk/).
- Import the official Connections.csv export for your first degree at no search cost: [how to export it](/blog/export-network-connections/).
