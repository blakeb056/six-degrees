If you found this page by searching for **Sixgrees** (with an *s*) or **Sixgree** (without one), you are in the right place. 

The project is **Sixgree** (often referred to as **Six Degrees** or **Sixgrees**), a free, local-first intelligence engine designed to map the hidden reach of your professional network.

Here is why the name exists, why people search for both, and what the tool does.

## Six degrees of separation

The name originates from the classic concept of **six degrees of separation**: the theory that anyone on Earth can be connected to any other person through an unbroken chain of six or fewer acquaintances.

In modern professional life, however, you rarely need six steps. For almost any target company, hiring manager, or founder you want to reach, you are almost always just **two degrees away**:

1. **First degree (1st)**: The people you know directly—colleagues, mentors, alumni, and former managers.
2. **Second degree (2nd)**: The thousands of people *they* know directly.

The tragedy of modern networking platforms is that while your 1st-degree connections are visible, your 2nd-degree paths are buried under algorithms, pagination limits, and ad feeds. You cannot see the direct bridge connecting you to the person you need to speak with.

## Why "Sixgree" and "Sixgrees"?

When we built the native desktop app and website, we combined *Six* and *Degrees* into the shorthand **Sixgree** (domain: [sixgree.com](https://sixgree.com/)). 

Because natural language makes it tempting to pluralize, many engineers, founders, and community members naturally type **Sixgrees** into search bars or GitHub. Both refer to the exact same software project:

- **The repository**: [github.com/blakeb056/six-degrees](https://github.com/blakeb056/six-degrees)
- **The official domain**: [sixgree.com](https://sixgree.com)
- **The desktop app**: Six Degrees / Sixgree for macOS and Linux.

## What Sixgree actually does

Regardless of how you spell it, the core mission remains the same: give you transparent, local control over your professional graph.

- **Paths & Separation**: Search any company (e.g., Stripe, OpenAI, Datadog) or role to immediately reveal which mutual contact bridges you to a decision-maker.
- **Power & Catalyst Scoring**: Identify the top 5% of connectors in your network who hold the keys to the most diverse industry circles.
- **100% Local & Private**: Sixgree stores everything in an offline SQLite database on your computer. Your contacts and network insights never leave your machine, and no SaaS subscription is required.
