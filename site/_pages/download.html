Troubleshooting

If something's not right

macOS says it can't check the app for malware
That's a copy from version 0.8.0 or earlier, before the app was signed. To keep using it, allow it once: close the message (not Move to Trash), then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
I'm not sure whether I have Apple Silicon or Intel
Apple menu → About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" is Apple Silicon; "Processor: …Intel…" is Intel.
The Scan page says Chrome is missing
Scanning drives Google Chrome, installed in Applications (on Linux, Google Chrome rather than Chromium). Everything else works without it: the sample network and the official CSV export need nothing.
How do I remove it completely?
Quit the app and drag Sixgree from Applications to the Trash. Then in Finder choose Go → Go to Folder…, paste ~/.six-degrees and move that folder to the Trash, and do the same for ~/Library/Application Support/Six Degrees.
