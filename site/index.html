The scanner

One click.
Paced. Safeguarded.

Every scan button works in one click, and each one is tested and held to the same rules.

  • Paced. Slow, Medium or Fast, measured in searches an hour.
  • Capped. Daily and monthly limits, with your usage on the Scan page.
  • Stops cleanly. At the first push-back it stops and says so, and Resume picks up where it left off.
  • No pop-ups. On a Mac it asks for App Management once, then leaves you alone.

The questions people ask first

Is it really free?

Yes. No paid tier, no account and no trial. Sixgree is open source under the MIT licence, and every line is on GitHub.

Who is Sixgree for?

Anyone on LinkedIn who wants to see their network rather than scroll it: people starting out with a handful of connections, people with thousands who want to see what they add up to, climbers looking for peers at the top, and founders looking for other founders, or a co-founder.

Does my network leave my computer?

No. There is no Sixgree server. Your network is one folder on your computer, and the app goes online only when you ask it to: LinkedIn while you scan, GitHub when you check for an update. Why local-first.

Will scanning get my LinkedIn account restricted?

It might, and we say so plainly. Scanning uses your own account in a Chrome window you sign into. It reads slowly, keeps to daily and monthly limits, and stops at the first sign of push-back. If you'd rather take no risk, import LinkedIn's own Connections.csv or try the sample. The details.

What do the tiers S, A, B, C and D mean?

Each person gets a power score from their title and their company, and the tiers group those scores: S is the top. Power ranks reachability, not people: it estimates how many doors someone can open, never what they're worth. The formula is open.

What's a bridge, and what does six degrees mean here?

A bridge is a connection whose circle you've scanned: everyone they know joins your map, two steps from you. Scan circles further out and the map keeps going, out to six degrees.

What does it run on?

macOS 13.5 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel, signed and notarized by Apple. Windows 10 or 11 and Linux are in beta. All downloads.
