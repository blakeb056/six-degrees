Plugins

Add only
what you want.

Sixgree ships without a scanner. Scanning is a plugin you choose to add, in one click from Settings → Plugins or right in the guided setup.

  • Off until you turn it on. Plugins start switched off, and Sixgree only goes online for them when you ask.
  • Checked before it installs. Each package is matched against the published list, and you confirm before anything is added.
  • Auto scan. Turns the pages by itself, paced and capped, and stops at the first push-back.
  • Manual scan. You scroll and click Next yourself, and it reads each page as you go.

The questions people ask first

Is it really free?

Yes. No paid tier, no account and no trial. Sixgree is open source under the MIT licence, and every line is on GitHub.

Who is Sixgree for?

Anyone who wants to see their professional connections rather than scroll through them: people starting out with a handful of connections, people with thousands who want to see what they add up to, climbers looking for peers at the top, and founders looking for other founders, or a co-founder.

Does my network leave my computer?

No. There is no Sixgree server. Your network is one folder on your computer, and the app goes online only when you ask it to (the platform when you scan, GitHub when you check for an update or browse plugins), apart from one request a day to GitHub for the newest version number (and, with plugins on, the plugin list), which you can turn off in Settings → Updates. Why local-first.

Could scanning restrict my account?

It can: automated scanning is against the platform's terms, and we say so plainly. In the cases we've recorded, the platform warned first and any hold was temporary, and both holds came from carrying on after a warning. Stay on Medium or Slow, let the scan windows hold you to 60 search pages at a time and 120 a day, and after a warning stop for a day. The scanner paces itself like a person and stops at the first push-back. To avoid scanning risk, import a Connections.csv export or try the sample. What really happens, and how to stay safe.

What do the tiers S, A, B, C and D mean?

Each person gets a power score from their title and their company, and the tiers group those scores: S is the top. Power ranks reachability, not people: it estimates how many doors someone can open, never what they're worth. The formula is open.

What's a bridge, and what does six degrees mean here?

A bridge is a connection whose circle you've scanned: everyone they know joins your map, two steps from you. Scan circles further out and the map keeps going, out to six degrees.

What does it run on?

macOS 13.5 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel, signed and notarized by Apple. Windows 10 or 11 and Linux are in beta. All downloads.
